Wybber Creator Program

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Share Wybber with your audience and earn a share of the subscriptions they buy. No sign-up fee: you earn a percentage of what the people you bring in actually spend.

Where things stand: Wybber isn’t in the App Store or Google Play yet. We’re taking early applications now, and the program is limited to 100 creators.

How it works

  1. Apply. Fill in the application form or email us. We review every application by hand.
  2. Get your link. Once approved, you get a personal invite code (it looks like WIN-XXXXXXXX) and a link to share.
  3. Share it. People who open your link see your code and can copy it with one tap. When they set up Wybber with your code, they get 14 days of Link free.
  4. Earn. When someone you brought in buys a subscription, a share of it is credited to you.

  • The share is calculated on the price the App Store or Google Play reports for each purchase, excluding VAT and sales tax. Where we haven’t confirmed the tax rules for a country yet, the share is held until we have.
  • On iPhone you earn on every renewal of the subscription. On Android you currently earn on the first purchase only; renewals don’t count yet.
  • A person is linked to you for life, and to only one creator.
  • Earnings start once Wybber passes 10,000 installs. Installs you bring in before then still count toward that number, but purchases made before it don’t earn a share afterwards.

Your privacy, and theirs

  • You never see who the people you brought in are: no names, profiles, photos or messages. We link them to you only through a number derived from their phone that carries no name, and you can’t tell who they are from it.
  • Your dashboard shows totals only: installs, earnings (pending and paid) and your network’s numbers.
  • What we collect about you as a creator (application and payout details), why, and for how long is set out in Privacy Policy section 13. It is never combined with dating-app data.

Your dashboard

Log in at https://influencer.wingerapp.com with a one-time login link. Until automatic login emails are switched on, our team sends you the link by hand. In the dashboard you can follow your installs and earnings and add your payout details.

Questions

When and how do I get paid?

Earnings are tracked as pending or paid. The payout schedule, method and minimum amount will be confirmed with you before the first payouts.

Do I need to issue an invoice or handle taxes?

That depends on your country and income. We’ll agree the details with you before the first payout.

Someone came from my link, but it doesn’t show. What should I do?

Write to us with the approximate date and we’ll look into it.

Apply

Apply online or email support@wybber.com with your name, your social media handles, roughly how big your audience is, and anything you’d like to add.
