Safety Center

Meeting new people should feel good. Here’s how Wybber helps keep you safe, and what you can do too. Every safety tool is free on every plan.

Who can see you?

Before you match, nobody sees your name or photo. After a match, they see your name, age band and city.

You open your bio and photos person by person: nobody sees them unless you open them to that person in the chat, and what you open goes only to their phone, encrypted.

A photo sent to you arrives first as an accept card. It stays hidden until you accept; if you decline, it is deleted from your phone and later photos from that person aren’t received. They aren’t notified.

Someone you haven’t replied to can send you at most 2 messages. They also can’t see whether you opened the chat until you reply.

Notifications never show a name or a message.

Your district is never shown to anyone: it is used only to order your own list.

Report and block

Open a chat and use Report or Block at the top, or tap Report or block in a match’s details.

Reports about violence, scams, harassment or inappropriate (sexual or graphic) content are sent with one tap and block the person at the same moment. Other reports (spam, a fake profile, something else) ask you to confirm first.

Blocking is silent: the other person isn’t told. They can no longer see you, match with you or message you. You can see and undo your blocks in Settings → Privacy and safety → Blocked users.

A person on our team reviews every report within 24 hours. They can take no action, send a warning or suspend the account. To protect you, we never tell the other person who reported them.

The liveness check

The optional liveness check asks you to blink and make another quick movement in front of your camera. It runs entirely on your phone, and the video and photo stay there.

Passing it earns a Liveness verified badge, and your matches are told you ran the check; nothing else (no photo, no face data) is shared. They see it as your phone’s own statement, not proof. On iPhone it can also check that you look like your profile photo. It isn’t an ID check, and no badge is a guarantee: always use your own judgement.

Guardian, on your phone

Before a message leaves your phone, Guardian checks it on your device. Harassment and hate speech are held back, and if a message asks you for money or sounds threatening, you see a one-time tip. Card and IBAN numbers, Turkish and Spanish ID numbers and verification codes are hidden to protect you from scams.

Sharing a phone number, email address or social media handle is up to you; the first time you do, you’ll see a short safety tip.

Guardian is an extra layer, not a replacement for you: reporting and blocking are your main tools.

Your chats stay private

Chats are end-to-end encrypted and go phone to phone. If the two phones can’t connect directly, the encrypted traffic passes through our own relay server, which sees IP addresses but never the content. We can’t read chats, so we can’t hand them over to anyone. What we can share, when the law requires it, is the limited account information listed in our Privacy Policy.

Meeting in person

  • Meet somewhere public the first time.
  • Tell a friend where you’re going and who you’re meeting.
  • Arrange your own way there and back.
  • Be careful with anyone who asks for money or pushes for personal details early on, and report them.
  • Trust your instincts. You can leave at any time.

In an emergency

Call your local emergency number. Wybber is not an emergency service, and reports are not monitored in real time.

Someone you think is under 18

Wybber is for adults only. In the app, report them with Report → Something else, and if you like, write the details to support@wybber.com. If you’re a parent and think your child is using Wybber, write to us.

We don’t check official ID, so we review with the information we have; accounts found to belong to someone under 18 are closed.
