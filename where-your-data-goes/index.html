Where does your data go?

Your information is in one of three places: on your phone, on the phone of the person you matched with, or on our server. Here’s what is where, in plain sight.

Stored on your phone

None of this is kept on our servers.

  • Your profile, bio and photos
  • Your answers to the preference questions, and how much each answer matters to you
  • Your chat history: encrypted on your phone; the person you’re talking to has their own copy
  • Working out how well you two fit: Wybber Intelligence does it on your phone
  • The video and photo of your liveness check

What you choose to share goes to the other person, as the next column shows.

Goes to the other person, encrypted

From your phone straight to theirs, end-to-end encrypted. We can’t read it.

  • In the first conversation: only the answers you choose to share
  • When you match: your name, age band and city (not your district)
  • When you open them: your bio and photos, person by person
  • The messages you write and the photos you send in a chat

Chats travel over the Tor network, so your IP address stays hidden. If you choose Wybber Relay, the encrypted packets pass through our server: never read, never stored, and your IP address isn’t shown to the other person. Calls connect directly. If the other person is offline, our server keeps a “message waiting” marker with no content.

What our server sees

The basics your account needs. We don’t sell it or use it for ads.

  • The account ID your phone creates, scrambled in most places; not your name, number or email
  • For matching: your gender and who you’re interested in, whether you’re after a relationship or friendship, your age band and city (and district, if you choose)
  • Your plan and purchase records
  • Your match records: who you matched with and when, under scrambled IDs; deleted after 180 days without chat activity
  • Who you blocked or reported, under scrambled IDs
  • The notification token we need to send notifications to your phone
  • Technical details to connect two phones. If you choose Wybber Relay, your encrypted packets pass through here too; never read, never stored
  • Like any online service, technical request logs that include your IP address

This is a summary, not the full list: section 3 of the Privacy Policy lists everything our server keeps and why, and section 5 how long.

When you delete your account, your account record on our server is deleted right away; the Support page explains what remains and for how long.
