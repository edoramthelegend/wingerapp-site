Wybber Ambassadors

Bring Wybber to your people.

On Wybber, profiles, photos and conversations are on people’s own phones, not on our servers. We’re looking for students, creators and community organisers who want to bring that idea to the people around them. Apply, and if it’s a fit, we’ll talk through the details.

Apply I have a question first

One programme for campuses and creators. Wybber is a dating and friendship app where what’s yours stays on your phone, built by a small, independent team. If people on your campus or online listen to you, apply to turn that into a partnership with Wybber. Ambassadors we sign a contract with are among the programme’s first members and share in its revenue. Every term is in the contract.

What you get

  • A share of revenue. Ambassadors we sign a contract with earn a share of the Wybber subscriptions they bring in. The rate and terms are in the contract.
  • A direct line to the team. You talk to the people building Wybber, and what you hear on campus or from your audience shapes what we build.
  • Be there from the start. You’ll be one of Wybber’s first ambassadors.
  • Your voice, not a script. No lines to memorise. Talk about Wybber your way; we make sure you have the facts.

Who we’re looking for

  • Students who are part of campus life: clubs, events, friend groups.
  • Creators whose audience trusts them. Trust matters more to us than follower count.
  • People who run a community: a club, an event series, a Discord server, a group chat.
  • Anyone 18 or over who talks about Wybber honestly and never pushes people.

We’re starting with campuses and communities in Türkiye.

After you apply

  1. A person on our team reads your application.
  2. If it’s a fit, we’ll email you to set up a short call.
  3. We share every detail of the programme in writing, in a contract. Nothing is binding until you sign.
  4. Once you’ve signed, you get your own invite code and a starter guide.

Quick answers

Do I earn money by inviting friends?
No. Revenue share is only for ambassadors with a signed contract. Sharing the app’s invite codes with friends earns nobody anything.
Do I have to pay anything?
No. Being an ambassador is free, and we’ll never ask you for money.
How many followers do I need?
There’s no minimum. What matters is that people trust you.
Why aren’t the details on this page?
We give you all of them in writing, in the contract, after we’ve talked, so you can read everything before you sign.
