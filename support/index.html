Delete your account

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. It takes effect on our server right away and can’t be undone. If you can’t open the app, restore your account with your 12 recovery words on a phone with Wybber and delete it there, or email us and we’ll delete it by hand once we’ve confirmed the account is yours.

Deleted from our server right away

Your account record (with your province, district, search settings and Travel Mode), your blocks, your notification token, your matches and match signals, your conversation records (who and when; we never have message content), chat reminders, the feedback you sent, usage records and app-integrity keys.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records: kept for financial records, with no set end date. The link to your account is cut for good and the phone ID on them is cleared.
  • Safety reports (filed by you or about you): both people’s IDs are replaced with a one-way pseudonym. A reviewed report is deleted 90 days after review, or 365 days if it led to a suspension.
  • Free-trial record: kept with no end date, so one phone can’t take a second trial. Only your phone’s scrambled ID and the trial’s dates remain.
  • Device record: your phone’s scrambled ID, kept for 90 days after the last account registered on that phone.
  • Referral-code and commission records: tied to your phone’s scrambled ID. The commission record is kept for 10 years because Turkish tax and commercial law require it (VUK Art. 253, TTK Art. 82); the referral-code record has no set end date.
  • Store receipts and suspension records: no set end date. Receipts stop the same Apple or Google notification from being processed twice; a suspension record proves a sanction was really applied.
  • Backups and server logs: nightly backups stay 7 days on our server (up to 30 days off-site, if an off-site copy is in place) and can hold a copy until they’re replaced. Server logs are overwritten once they reach about 50 MB.

On your phone, the app removes your profile, answers, photos, chats and keys once our server has confirmed the deletion. It deletes files but doesn’t overwrite them, on iPhone one encryption key can outlive the app (the app tells you if it couldn’t remove it), and it can’t reach copies outside the app, such as phone backups or messages already delivered to someone else.

The full list and the legal bases are in Privacy Policy sections 5 and 6.

Common questions

What exactly is deleted when I delete my account?

Your account, profile, blocks, location and chat and match records are deleted from our server, and your profile, photos, answers and chats are removed from your phone. Purchase records and safety reports are kept, but with the link to you permanently cut, for financial audits and to keep moderation fair. See Privacy Policy section 6.

How do I get a copy of my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Privacy and safety → Download your data. You’ll get one file with what our server holds about your account and what’s stored on your phone. Your message history isn’t included, because it isn’t kept on our servers.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the chat and use Report or Block at the top, or tap Report or block in the match’s details. More in the Safety Center.

Are my photos uploaded to your servers?

No. Your photos, profile and the liveness-check video aren’t uploaded to our servers.

How do I stop my Wybber talking for me?

Go to Settings → Matching and turn off Let my Wybber talk for me. Any conversation in progress stops right away.

How do I cancel a subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play. Cancel in your phone’s subscription settings at least 24 hours before it renews. Refunds follow Apple’s or Google’s own rules.

I moved to a new phone. How do I get my account back?

Use your recovery phrase when you set up Wybber on the new phone, or move your account with a QR code while you still have the old phone.

My account was suspended. What can I do?

If you think it was a mistake, write to support@wybber.com. A person on our team will look at it again.
