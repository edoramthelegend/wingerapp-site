Who sees what?

You decide what to open, and to whom. At every step it’s clear what the other person sees.

  1. Before a match

    Nobody sees your name or photo. The assistants compare only the answers you chose to share.

  2. Once you match

    They see your name, age band and city. You both see what you have in common.

  3. When you open up

    You open your bio and photo one person at a time. A photo arrives first as a card to accept; it stays hidden until they do.

How it works

Three steps. Your assistant does the tiring part. You get the fun part.

  1. 1

    More

What we can’t read, we can’t hand over.

What you choose to share goes to the other person end-to-end encrypted; your messages are stored on your phone and on the phone of the person you’re talking to, not with us.

To introduce you, our server knows basics like your city and age band. No ads, and we never sell your data.

On your phone

  • Your profile, bio and photos
  • Your answers and preferences
  • Your chat history, encrypted

To the other person, encrypted

  • The answers you choose to share
  • Once you match: your name, age band and city
  • When you open them: your bio and photos

On our server

  • A random account number your phone creates
  • Your city, age band and plan
  • A record of who you matched with; never what you wrote

They never even see your IP address. Chats go over the Tor network by default. Want it faster? Choose Wybber Relay: messages pass through encrypted, never read or stored, and your IP address stays hidden from the other person.

Common questions

When and where does Wybber launch?

Very soon. Wybber opens city by city; if yours isn’t open yet, set up your profile and you’ll hear when it opens. Don’t miss launch day: join the list.

Does my assistant send messages for me?

No. Your assistant has the first conversation: it compares the answers you chose to share and brings you the result. It never writes messages in your name; once you match, the chat is all yours.

Who can see my photo?

Only the people you open it to. Before a match nobody sees it, and after one you open it to one person at a time. It goes phone to phone, encrypted, and stays hidden until they accept it.

Where is my information?

Your profile, photos, answers and chats are on your phone, not on our servers. To introduce you, our server keeps basics like your city and age band. Where does your data go? shows what is where.

What can I do on the free plan?

Your assistant runs one round for you every week, and one match a week is shown in full. Reading and answering messages, reporting, blocking and the liveness check are free on every plan.

What happens to my stuff if I switch phones?

If you still have your old phone, move everything over with a QR code on the same Wi-Fi: your profile, photos and chats included. Without it, your 12 recovery words bring back your account and plan; your past messages and photos were only on the old phone, so they don’t come back.
