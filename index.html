Dating where your data stays yours

Most dating apps work by collecting everything. WingerApp is built the other way round.

  • Your data stays on your device

    Your profile, bio, photos and chats live on your phone. They are never uploaded to our servers.

  • Matching runs on your phone

    Compatibility is worked out on your device. Your Winger only shares the category answers you have allowed, end-to-end encrypted, with a candidate’s device.

  • The server sees as little as possible

    Hashed identifiers, a few coarse registration details and the outcome of an introduction. Never your photos, your messages or your answers.

How it works

From sign-up to a first message, in four steps.

  1. 1

    Build your profile on your phone

    Answer a short, chat-style onboarding. Your name is optional; your photos and bio stay on your device.

  2. 2

    Meet your Winger

    Your Winger is the on-device assistant that knows what you are looking for. It only talks to other people’s Wingers if you switch that on in Settings. It is off by default and you can turn it off at any time.

  3. 3

    Introductions happen phone to phone

    When it is on, Wingers exchange only the category answers you have chosen to share, end-to-end encrypted and between the two devices. Our server never sees them.

  4. 4

    You decide whether to talk

    If it is a mutual fit you get a match. Details are revealed gradually rather than all at once, and the chat is end-to-end encrypted.

What is in the app

  • On-device matching

    Compatibility is computed on your phone, not on a server.

  • Winger conversations, opt-in

    “Let my Winger talk for me” stays off until you switch it on, and you choose which categories your Winger may answer.

  • Encrypted chat

    Messages are encrypted on your device and can only be read by the person you are talking to.

  • Optional selfie check

    A live selfie check that runs entirely on your phone. The image does not go to our servers.

  • Recovery phrase and phone transfer

    Your identity is derived from a recovery phrase, so you can restore it on another device or move to a new phone with a QR code.

  • Report and block

    Report or block anyone in a tap. Serious categories do both at once.

What stays where

The honest version of “we care about your privacy”. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Stays on your phone

  • Your profile, bio and photos
  • Your chats
  • The image from the optional selfie check
  • The matching calculations
  • Answers you have not allowed to be shared

Our server sees

  • Irreversibly hashed identifiers, not names or emails
  • Basic registration details: gender and who you are interested in, an age band, and a coarse location
  • The outcome of an introduction: no match, or match
  • Subscription status from the App Store or Google Play, and your notification token

Built with safety in mind

WingerApp is rated 17+ and publishes a Safety and Moderation Policy. Every profile and chat has Report and Block, and someone you block can no longer see you or contact you.
