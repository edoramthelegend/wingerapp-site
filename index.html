Here is exactly what stays where.

Messages go phone to phone, end-to-end encrypted, and aren’t kept on our servers; neither are your photos and answers. “On your phone” means your chat isn’t written to a central server; what you choose to share goes only to the other person’s phone, encrypted. We can’t read it; no ads or trackers in the app, and we don’t sell your data.

How? Here it is in plain words.

  • Wybber gets to know you on your phone

    You say what matters; your Wybber keeps it on your phone and uses it for you, not for ads. How well you two fit is worked out on your phone. Your Wybber shares only the topics you allow, encrypted, straight to the other person’s phone; we can’t read them.

  • Chats go phone to phone

    Messages are end-to-end encrypted and go phone to phone. If the two phones can’t connect directly, the encrypted traffic passes through our own relay server, which sees IP addresses but never the content. Messages aren’t stored on our servers, and we can’t read them.

  • What’s on our server?

    The basics your account needs to work, for example a random account number your phone creates, your age band, your city and your plan. Your name, photos, answers and messages aren’t kept there. The full list is in the Privacy Policy.

Not kept on our servers

  • Your profile, bio and photos: shared only with your matches, phone to phone. You open your bio and photos person by person; a photo you open reaches the other person first as an accept card and stays hidden until they accept; if they decline, it is deleted from their phone and later photos are neither shown to them nor kept
  • Your answers and preferences: Wybbers compare what matters to you in categories, never in your own words
  • Your chats: phone to phone, end-to-end encrypted
  • The video and photo from your liveness check: only on your phone
  • Working out how well you two fit: on your phone

What our servers keep

  • A random account number your phone creates, not your name, email or phone number
  • Basics for matching: your gender and who you’re interested in, an age band and your city
  • Your district (optional): used only to order your list, people in that district first, then the rest of your city. It is never shown to anyone else, and with no district chosen there is no ordering
  • The district and country the sign-up form sends: stored, but not used for matching; we plan to remove them
  • Your match records: who you matched and connected with, as scrambled IDs, and when; never what you wrote
  • Who you blocked or reported, as scrambled IDs
  • The dates you opened the app (no times)
  • Like any online service, technical request logs that include your IP address
  • Your subscription status from the App Store or Google Play, and the notification token we need to send notifications to your phone

This is a summary; section 3 of the Privacy Policy lists everything our server keeps and why.

How it works

Three steps. Your Wybber does the heavy lifting; you show up for the good part.

    Your Wybber does the first talk

    Tell it what matters to you and your AI relationship assistant gets to know what you’re looking for. Relationship, new friends or both: you choose what you’re after. Once you say OK, your Wybber has the first conversation with other people’s Wybbers for you: phone to phone, encrypted, sharing only the answers you choose. You do the chatting; your Wybber never writes a word to your match in your name.

Plans

Start free. Upgrade when you want your Wybber to run more rounds. In each round, your Wybber has the first talk with up to 10 people.

Safety is free on every plan: reading and answering messages, reporting, blocking, the liveness check and deleting your account.

  • Free

    Get started

    • 1 round a week
    • Up to 10 people per round
    • 1 match a week shown in full
    • In chats you both wrote in, up to 5 messages in a row before a reply

  • Spark

    A round every day

    • 7 rounds a week · at most 1 a day
    • Up to 10 people per round
    • All your matches shown
    • In chats you both wrote in, up to 25 messages in a row before a reply

  • Link

    More rounds and Travel Mode

    • 21 rounds a week · at most 3 a day
    • Up to 10 people per round
    • All your matches shown
    • In chats you both wrote in, no limit on messages before a reply
    • Travel Mode, up to 14 days at a time
    • Coming soon: Wybber Intelligence, your AI relationship assistant. On supported phones it writes your round summaries and reply ideas right on your phone; included in this plan when it launches

  • Infinity

    The most rounds

    • 50 rounds a week
    • Up to 10 people per round
    • All your matches shown
    • In chats you both wrote in, no limit on messages before a reply
    • Travel Mode, up to 14 days at a time
    • Coming soon: Wybber Intelligence, your AI relationship assistant. On supported phones it writes your round summaries and reply ideas right on your phone; included in this plan when it launches

New to Wybber? Try Link free for 7 days. With a creator’s invite code it’s 14 days on Android; on iPhone the code links you to the creator and you get the standard 7-day trial. The trial starts with your first round.

Until the other person has replied to you, you can send at most 2 messages on every plan; message packs also only work in chats you both wrote in.

Message packs are also available in the app. Prices are shown in the App Store or Google Play, in your currency.

Travel Mode

Going somewhere? Turn on Travel Mode and be matched in another city, in any country, for up to 14 days. People there see you, and you see them. When it ends, you’re back in your own city.

Included in Link and Infinity. On the other plans you’re matched in the city you live in.

Safety comes first, and it’s free

Every safety tool is included in every plan.

  • Report and block

    Report or block someone from any chat or match. Reports about violence, scams, harassment or inappropriate (sexual or graphic) content block them at the same moment. A person reviews every report within 24 hours.

  • Liveness check

    An optional short liveness check that runs on your phone and earns a “Liveness verified” badge; your matches are told you did it. The video stays on your phone.

  • Guardian

    Guardian checks your messages on your phone before they’re sent: it holds back harassment and hate speech, shows you a one-time tip when a message asks you for money or sounds threatening, and hides card and IBAN numbers, Turkish and Spanish ID numbers and verification codes.

  • Adults only

    Wybber is for people aged 18 and over.

Questions and answers

When can I download Wybber?
Soon. Wybber is getting ready for the App Store and Google Play, and isn’t on either yet. Want to hear when it launches? Tap here: one email is enough.
Is Wybber available in my city?
Wybber opens city by city. Until your city opens you can complete your profile and invite friends, and if you allowed notifications you’ll hear when your city opens. In a newly opened city, rounds may be free for a while.
Is it only for dating?
No. On Wybber you can look for a relationship, friendship, or both. If you choose friendship, you aren’t asked about children, marriage or intimacy.
What does “your Wybber does the first talk” mean?
Your Wybber runs on your phone. Once you turn it on, it has short, structured conversations with other people’s Wybbers, comparing answers on the topics you’ve chosen to share. It doesn’t write your messages or chat in your name: once you match, the conversation is yours.
Is it a chatbot like ChatGPT?
No. Your Wybber is AI that runs on your phone using clear rules, not a chatbot in the cloud. Who you match with comes down to the bar you each set. We’re also preparing optional Wybber Intelligence: on supported phones it will also run on your phone and describe your rounds in plain words. It will be included in Link and Infinity and isn’t out yet. It switches on in each language once that language passes its tests, and it never decides who you match with.
What is Wybber Intelligence?
Wybber Intelligence is your AI relationship assistant. It sums up your Wybber’s rounds in plain words and suggests replies in your chats. It runs on your phone, on supported phones only; it never decides who you match with, and a suggestion is only sent if you pick it and send it yourself. It comes with Link and Infinity and isn’t sold separately. It isn’t out yet, it’s coming soon: it switches on once it passes its tests and proves it works well on the phone; each language switches on once it passes its own tests. Supported phones: iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, the iPhone 16 lineup and later; iPhone 14 Pro, 14 Pro Max, iPhone 15 and 15 Plus only if the speed we measure on the phone is good enough; and selected Android phones.
Will I see a compatibility score?
No, there are no numbers or percentages. A round result is a word: worth a try, a fit, or a strong fit. A match means both of your bars were cleared, and the app shows you what you have in common.
How many messages can I send before they reply?
At most 2 until the other person replies to you, on every plan and every message pack. Once they reply, your plan’s limit applies. Until they reply you can’t see whether they opened the chat; no “opened” or “active” signal reaches you before a reply.
Can I share my phone number in a chat?
Yes. Sharing a phone number, email address or social media handle is up to you; the first time you do, you’ll see a short safety tip. Card and IBAN numbers, Turkish and Spanish ID numbers and verification codes are hidden to protect you from scams.
Do I need an email address or phone number?
No. When you sign up you get 12 recovery words instead. Keep them safe: they’re how you get your account back on a new phone. While you still have your old phone, you can also move your account, chats included, with a QR code.
Do you store my photos or messages?
No. Your photos, profile, answers and chats aren’t kept on our servers; chats go phone to phone, end-to-end encrypted. Our servers keep the basics your account needs; the Privacy Policy lists exactly what.
What is a Wybber ambassador?
Someone who introduces Wybber on their campus or to their audience. You can apply on the Wybber Ambassadors page.
Which languages does Wybber speak?
English and Turkish. Spanish is next.
How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. If you can’t open the app, email us. Details are on the Support page.
