Why Wybber?

  • It goes first. You take it from there.

    Wybber Intelligence is your AI relationship assistant. It gets to know you on your phone and has the first conversation with theirs, so you don’t have to. Not a fit? Nobody’s time wasted. A fit? It introduces you.

  • No swiping. No manipulation.

    No shop window, no endless feed, no algorithm built to keep you hooked. Wybber isn’t here to keep you on screen; it’s here to introduce you to someone. The result is a word, never a score.

  • Your chats aren’t ours to keep

    Your messages go phone to phone, end-to-end encrypted. There’s no chat archive piling up on our servers, and even we can’t read them.

  • You’re not the product

    Wybber makes money from plans, not ads. No ads. We don’t sell your data. We don’t even ask for your email or phone number.

  • Safety is free. On every plan.

    Report or block anyone, and a real person reviews every report within 24 hours. Guardian stops harassment and hate speech before it’s even sent. Want to show you’re real? Take the liveness check and get the badge.

We have no chat archive to steal.

Your profile, your photos and every conversation are on your phone, not on our servers. Chats go phone to phone, end-to-end encrypted, and are stored, encrypted, on your phone and on the phone of the person you’re talking to; they don’t pile up on our servers. We can’t read them; no ads or trackers in the app, and we don’t sell your data.

How? In three points.

  • It gets to know you on your phone

    You say what matters; Wybber Intelligence learns it on your phone and uses it for you, not for ads. How well you two fit is worked out on your phone too. Only the topics you choose to share go to the other person, encrypted, straight to their phone.

  • Phone to phone

    Messages are end-to-end encrypted and travel over the Tor network, so your IP address stays hidden. Want it faster? Choose Wybber Relay: the encrypted packets pass through our server but are never read or stored, and your IP address isn’t shared with the other person. Calls connect directly.

  • What’s on our server?

    The basics for introducing you and running your account: a random account number your phone creates, your age band, your city, your plan. Your name, photos, answers and messages aren’t there. The full list is in the Privacy Policy.

See exactly what stays where

Not kept on our servers

  • Your profile, bio and photos: shared only with your matches, phone to phone. You open your bio and photos person by person; a photo you open reaches the other person first as an accept card and stays hidden until they accept; if they decline, it is deleted from their phone and later photos are neither shown to them nor kept
  • Your answers and preferences: Wybbers compare what matters to you in categories, never in your own words
  • Your chats: phone to phone, end-to-end encrypted
  • The video and photo from your liveness check: only on your phone
  • Working out how well you two fit: on your phone

What our servers keep

  • A random account number your phone creates, not your name, email or phone number
  • Basics for matching: your gender and who you’re interested in, an age band and your city
  • Your district (optional): used only to order your list, people in that district first, then the rest of your city. It is never shown to anyone else, and with no district chosen there is no ordering
  • The district and country the sign-up form sends: stored, but not used for matching; we plan to remove them
  • Your match records: who you matched and connected with, as scrambled IDs, and when; never what you wrote
  • Who you blocked or reported, as scrambled IDs
  • The dates you opened the app (no times)
  • Like any online service, technical request logs that include your IP address
  • Your subscription status from the App Store or Google Play, and the notification token we need to send notifications to your phone

This is a summary; section 3 of the Privacy Policy lists everything our server keeps and why.

Safety is free on every plan: reading and answering messages, reporting, blocking, the liveness check and deleting your account.

Travel Mode

Off somewhere? Turn on Travel Mode and match wherever you’re headed, anywhere in the world, for up to 14 days. People there see you, and you see them. When it ends, you’re back in your own city.

Included in Link and Infinity. On the other plans you’re matched in the city you live in.

Safety first. And it’s free.

Every safety tool, on every plan, from day one.

  • Report and block

    Report or block someone from any chat or match. Reports about violence, scams, harassment or inappropriate (sexual or graphic) content block them at the same moment. A person reviews every report within 24 hours.

  • Liveness check

    An optional short liveness check that runs on your phone and earns a “Liveness verified” badge; your matches are told you did it. The video stays on your phone.

  • Guardian

    Guardian checks your messages on your phone before they’re sent: it holds back harassment and hate speech, shows you a one-time tip when a message asks you for money or sounds threatening, and hides card and IBAN numbers, Turkish and Spanish ID numbers and verification codes.

  • Adults only

    Wybber is for people aged 18 and over.

Common questions

When can I download Wybber?

Soon. Wybber is getting ready for the App Store and Google Play. Want to be the first to know? Tap here: one email is enough.

Is Wybber available in my city?

Wybber opens city by city. If yours isn’t open yet, set up your profile and bring your friends; if you allowed notifications, you’ll hear the moment it opens. In a newly opened city, rounds may be free for a while.

Is it only for dating?

No. A relationship, friendship, or both: your call. Choose friendship and the questions about children, marriage and intimacy never come up.

What does “your Wybber has the first conversation” mean?

Your Wybber runs on your phone. Once you turn it on, it has short, structured conversations with other people’s Wybbers, comparing answers on the topics you’ve chosen to share. It doesn’t write your messages or chat in your name: once you match, the conversation is yours.

Is it a chatbot in the cloud?

No. Wybber Intelligence is AI that runs on your phone and learns on your phone. Its job isn’t to chat with you, it’s to introduce you to the right person. Who you match with comes down to the bar you each set.

What is Wybber Intelligence?

Your AI relationship assistant. On every plan, from day one. It runs on your phone and learns on your phone: who might suit you better, and which conversation ideas work for you. It has the first conversation too; a match happens when you both clear each other’s bar. Soon it will start writing as well: summing up your rounds in plain words and suggesting replies in your chats; a suggestion is only sent if you pick it and send it yourself. That writing part will come with Link and Infinity and switches on in each language once that language passes its tests. Supported phones for the writing part: iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, the iPhone 16 lineup and later; iPhone 14 Pro, 14 Pro Max, iPhone 15 and 15 Plus only if the speed we measure on the phone is good enough; and selected Android phones.

Do I have to pay extra for Wybber Intelligence?

No. The first conversation, the learning on your phone and the conversation ideas are on every plan. The writing part that’s coming (round summaries and reply ideas) will come with Link and Infinity; it isn’t sold separately.

Why no swiping?

Because the first conversation does the sorting. In each round your Wybber talks to a handful of other Wybbers and introduces you only if you both clear each other’s bar. No shop window: nobody can swipe or browse their way to you.

Will I see a compatibility score?

No. No numbers, no percentages. A round result is one word: worth a try, a fit, or a strong fit. If you matched, you both cleared each other’s bar, and the app shows you what you have in common.

How many messages can I send before they reply?

At most 2 until the other person replies to you, on every plan and every message pack. Once they reply, your plan’s limit applies. Until they reply you can’t see whether they opened the chat; no “opened” or “active” signal reaches you before a reply.

Can I share my phone number in a chat?

Yes. Sharing a phone number, email address or social media handle is up to you; the first time you do, you’ll see a short safety tip. Card and IBAN numbers, Turkish and Spanish ID numbers and verification codes are hidden to protect you from scams.

Do I need an email address or phone number?

No. When you sign up you get 12 recovery words instead. Keep them safe: they’re how you get your account back on a new phone. While you still have your old phone, you can also move your account, chats included, with a QR code.

Do you store my photos or messages?

No. Your photos, profile, answers and chats aren’t kept on our servers; chats go phone to phone, end-to-end encrypted. Our servers keep the basics your account needs; Where does your data go? shows what goes where, and the Privacy Policy lists all of it.

If your server were attacked, would my chats leak?

There are no chats or photos stored on our servers, so there’s no chat history there to steal. What our server does hold is the basics your account needs, like your city, age band, plan and match records under scrambled IDs; the Privacy Policy lists all of it. Your chats are stored encrypted on your phone, so keeping your phone locked matters too.

How does Wybber make money?

From plans. You start free; if you want more rounds, you move to Spark, Link or Infinity. No ads, and we don’t sell your data. You’re not the product here.

Will Wybber ever ask me for money or my recovery words?

No. Wybber never asks you for money, card details or your 12 recovery words; purchases only go through the App Store or Google Play. Anyone who asks for them isn’t Wybber: don’t share anything, and write to support@wybber.com.

What is a Wybber ambassador?

Someone who introduces Wybber on their campus or to their audience. You can apply on the Wybber Ambassadors page.

Which languages does Wybber speak?

English and Turkish. Spanish is next.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. If you can’t open the app, email us. Details are on the Support page.
