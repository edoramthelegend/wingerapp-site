How it works

  • Your data stays on your device

    Your profile, photos, and chats stay on your device — never sent to a server.

  • Matching is computed on your device

    Matching runs on your device; your Winger shares only the category answers you've allowed, end-to-end encrypted, directly with a candidate's device.

  • The server only sees the outcome

    The server only ever sees your irreversibly hashed identity and the outcome (no match / match).
