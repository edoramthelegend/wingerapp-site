2. Age Requirement — 18+

Wybber is for people 18 years of age or older only. The youngest age-band option in the registration flow is 18-22; no younger option is offered. Accounts found to belong to someone under 18 are closed immediately. Registration by a minor is not permitted even with parental/guardian consent.

3. Account and Identity

  • Your account is a cryptographic key pair created on your device (Ed25519); there is no username or password.
  • You represent that the information you provide (gender, age band, location, etc.) is accurate. Creating a fake or misleading profile violates these Terms (see §5, §6 — "fake profile" is a report category).
  • You are responsible for your account's security, including the physical security of your device. Your account's private key is held only on your device; keep your 12 recovery words safe — if you lose both your device and your recovery words, you may lose access to your account.
  • Your account is opened at the start of the sign-up screens, right after the KVKK and consent step; until you finish sign-up it is not visible to others. If you leave sign-up unfinished and take no step for 90 days, your account is deleted (see Privacy Policy §1.4).
  • During the launch phase an invite code may be required to get into Wybber. Whether invite-only entry is on is decided by our server; this requirement can be lifted after the launch phase. Sharing an invite code earns neither you nor the person who invited you anything; of the invitations, only campus ambassadors who have a contract with the Company receive a share (see §7 and Privacy Policy §1.5).

4. Nature of the Service

  • Wybber is a dating app built on on-device computation. Your name, bio, photos, preference answers and messages aren't kept on our servers: they're with you, not with us. When you match, your name — and, once you agree, your bio and photo — travels end-to-end encrypted only to your match's phone; your messages also go phone to phone, end-to-end encrypted (when the phones can't connect directly, the encrypted packets pass through our relay server, which neither reads nor stores their content). To find candidates, our server keeps your gender, the gender you're interested in, what you're looking for (relationship/friendship), your city and your age range; everything our server keeps, and why, is listed in §3 of our Privacy Policy.
  • "Wybber negotiation" (Settings → Matching → "Let my Wybber talk for me") is an optional feature that uses an explicit, deterministic/rule-based matching engine (Wybber Intelligence plays no part in this decision); its only possible outcomes are "no match" or "match." It is not human advice or a guarantee.
  • Face verification is an experimental (beta) feature; it runs entirely on-device and does not claim the reliability of commercial identity-verification systems.
  • Wybber does not guarantee any user's identity, intentions, or trustworthiness. You must exercise your own judgment and caution around meeting people (see the Safety and Moderation Policy).

5. Prohibited Conduct

Wybber has zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, fraud and objectionable content; accounts engaging in such conduct may be suspended or terminated without warning. You can report and block an abusive person or content with a single tap inside the app (see §6).

The following are strictly prohibited and may result in account termination and/or legal action:

  • Harassment, threats, bullying, hate speech.
  • Creating a fake profile or impersonating another person.
  • Fraud, requests for money/gifts, financial exploitation ("romance scams").
  • Attempting to contact a minor, or sharing content involving anyone under 18.
  • Sharing inappropriate/obscene content, or non-consensual imagery.
  • Using the App for commercial spam, advertising, or unauthorized data collection.
  • Abusing the system: artificially multiplying accounts, unauthorized API access, or attempting to bypass safety controls (the Guardian policy engine, the report/block system).
  • Promoting violence or illegal activity.

The Community Guidelines explain these rules in plain words with examples; the Safety and Moderation Policy describes the full category list and reporting flow.

6. Moderation, Reporting, and Blocking

  • You can report or block any user from within the App (in a chat, the ⋯ menu → "Report" / "Block"; or "Report or block" in a match's details). High-risk categories (violence, scam, harassment) both report and block in a single tap.
  • Reports are stored on the server and every report is reviewed by a Wybber operator within 24 hours of reaching it; the operator sees only the category, the time and hashed identities — never message content — and can take no action, warn the reported user, or suspend the account (see the Safety and Moderation Policy §3). Blocking protects you immediately: a person you block can no longer see you, match with you, or message you. The report channel is not an emergency service; in an emergency, call your local emergency services. Even if you delete your account, report records may continue to be retained; in them, the deleted account's ID is replaced with a pseudonym that only our server key can link back to it (see the Privacy Policy §5-6).
  • The Company reserves the right to suspend or terminate any account it reasonably believes has violated these Terms.

7. In-App Purchases (IAP)

  • Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed through the Apple App Store / Google Play; pricing is whatever is displayed by the store at the time of purchase.
  • Subscriptions auto-renew unless you cancel through your store account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.
  • Refund requests are subject to Apple's/Google's own refund policies; the Company does not process refunds directly (purchase-platform infrastructure is used).
  • The raw content of your purchase receipt is never stored by the Company; only the verification result and a hash of the receipt are stored (see the Privacy Policy §3).
  • Campus ambassadors and lifetime Infinity. Campus ambassadors who have a contract with the Company, and the first 5 people who use an ambassador's invite code directly, use the Infinity plan for life under that contract. This is not a purchase: no store transaction, receipt or payment arises. The entitlement is tied to your account; it ends if you delete your account, and nobody else takes its place.
  • City opening and regional free mode. Wybber rounds open in a city once enough active users are there; until then the city is on a waiting list. After a city opens, and until its number of active users reaches a higher threshold, the limits of the paid plans do not apply. Both thresholds are calculated from our server's active-account counts per city and may change. A round is 10 people; where your personal pool of candidates is thin it is run with 8, and thin-pool rounds do not use up your plan's round allowance. Daily safety limits always apply.
